\subsection{Overview}\label{overview}

This report presents subnational prevalence estimates for 20 indicators in Nigeria using DHS data. A summary of all indicators and survey information is provided on the following page.

For each indicator, two sets of estimates are shown based on the latest and the previous DHS survey, when available. Details on the statistical methods used can be found at \url{https://sae4health.stat.uw.edu/method/approach_overview/}. The following outputs are included:

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Sampling information}\\
  Number of clusters, samples, and events by Admin-1 and Admin-2 areas.
\item
  \textbf{Admin-1 maps}\\
  Weighted estimates at the Admin-1 level (without spatial smoothing). For each area, we present the point estimate, the width of the 90\% uncertainty interval, and the probability that prevalence exceeds the national prevalence estimate for each survey.
\item
  \textbf{Admin-2 maps}\\
  Spatially smoothed estimates at the Admin-2 level. Spatial smoothing is usually necessary to account for sparse data. The same set of maps as Admin-1 are presented.
\item
  \textbf{Admin-1 vs.~Admin-2 comparison}\\
  Side-by-side comparison of prevalence at Admin-1 and Admin-2. To improve readability, uncertainty intervals are omitted. Because Admin-2 estimates are spatially smoothed, the Admin-1 estimate may fall outside the range of the corresponding Admin-2 estimates, especially when prevalence is near 0\% or 100\%.
\item
  \textbf{Scatter plots (Admin-2)}\\
  Comparison of weighted (unsmoothed) and smoothed Admin-2 estimates. Red dots indicate areas without valid weighted estimates. Smoothed estimates generally show shrinkage toward the mean and narrower uncertainty intervals. If the smoothed estimates appear flat, this may indicate overshrinkage due to extreme data sparsity; such results should be interpreted with caution.
\item
  \textbf{Ridge plots (Admin-1)}\\
  Visualization of the latest Admin-1 prevalence distributions and changes since the previous survey.
\item
  \textbf{Interval plots (Admin-1 and Admin-2)}\\
  Prevalence estimates with uncertainty intervals for both the most recent and previous surveys.
\end{itemize}
